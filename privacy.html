AI Gatehouse Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: AI Gatehouse answers the gate of a gated community. It keeps the resident list, guest passes and a record of visits in the community's own cloud, so that residents can answer visitors from their phones. Number plates and face data stay on the device at the gate and are not sent to that cloud. What a visitor says at the kiosk goes to an AI service so that the gate can reply. We do not sell data, show advertising or use analytics in the app.

Who is responsible

AI Gatehouse is made by Melih Ercan, an individual developer in Türkiye ("we"). You can reach us at aigatehouse.support@gmail.com.

Each community (usually its homeowners' association) decides to use AI Gatehouse, adds its residents and sets its gate rules. It is responsible for the data of its residents and visitors, and we handle that data on its behalf. Each community has its own separate cloud and database.

Residents

The community manager enters, and the community's cloud keeps:

  • your name, unit and address, and your email and phone number if the manager adds them;
  • for each phone you pair: the name the phone gives itself (for example "Maria's iPhone"), when it was paired and when it was last in touch, and a push token, which is how a notification reaches it when the app is closed;
  • the guest passes you or the manager make: the guest's name, the code and when it is valid;
  • your visits: a short summary of who came and why, the times, and your answer.

The gate's device keeps a copy of the resident list and valid passes so that it can work when the internet is down. It may also keep a list of residents' number plates.

Visitors

  • Talking to the kiosk. The kiosk is an AI voice. What you say, and the resident details it needs to find the person you are visiting, go to the AI service the community chose, while you are talking: xAI (Grok) by default, Anthropic (Claude) as an alternative, or a model running on the gate's device, in which case nothing leaves it. We do not record your voice. The written conversation exists only in the gate device's memory and is not saved to a file. The AI service handles what it receives under its own terms.
  • The summary. The kiosk writes one or two sentences about who you are and why you came, for the resident. That summary is kept in the visit record, and the manager can see it too.
  • Guest passes. If a resident made you a pass, your name is kept with it.
  • Your number plate. Where the gate has a plate camera, it reads the plate of every vehicle that comes to the gate. The plate text, time and how sure the reading was are kept on the gate's device for 7 days, unless the community sets another period. A community can also choose to keep the picture behind a plate reading, for the same period.

Face recognition

Face recognition is off unless a community turns it on, and it is not used everywhere. Where it is on, a camera at a pedestrian door checks the faces that come to it against a list of people enrolled by the community. The community must enrol only people who have agreed. The list holds a numeric template of each face, not a photo, and it stays on the gate's device. A face that does not match is not added to any list. The gate's log keeps the result of each check for 7 days.

Community managers

Managers sign in to the portal with a Microsoft account. Microsoft handles the sign-in and tells the portal who you are and that you are allowed in. Alerts about the gate are emailed to the contacts the community gave, through Microsoft Azure.

The phone app

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code, and only when you press Scan. The code is read on your phone.
  • Notifications: to tell you someone is at the gate. On Android the app can also ring over the lock screen, like a call.
  • The app keeps its sign-in token in the phone's secure storage. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code.

Who else handles the data

  • Microsoft Azure hosts each community's cloud and database, in the United States. It also keeps technical logs of requests and errors, for 30 days.
  • Google, Apple and Microsoft deliver notifications to phones. A notification carries the visitor summary and the community's name.
  • xAI or Anthropic, as described under Visitors.
  • The community: its managers can see residents, passes, phones and visits.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We may give it to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long data is kept

  • The gate's event log, with plate readings and face check results, and any plate pictures: 7 days, unless the community sets another period.
  • Visit records and alerts: 12 months.
  • Guest passes: 30 days after they end.
  • A paired phone and its push token: until you sign out of the app or the manager removes the phone. Signing out removes both at once.
  • Resident details: until the manager removes you, which also removes your phones, passes and visit records.

Database backups are kept for 7 days. When a community stops using AI Gatehouse, its cloud and database are deleted within 30 days, and their backups expire within 7 days after that.

Security

Connections to the cloud are encrypted. Phone tokens, pairing codes and the gate's key are kept only as hashes. Push service keys are kept in a key vault. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell the community if a breach affects its data.

Children

The app is for residents and managers, not for children under 13. A child may still come to a gate, and the gate treats them as any other visitor.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or US state privacy laws may give you the right to see, correct or delete your personal data. Ask your community manager first, as the community holds your data, or write to us at the address above and we will help.

Changes

If AI Gatehouse starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
