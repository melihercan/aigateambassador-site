AI Gate Ambassador

AI Gate Ambassador answers the gate of your community and rings your phone when someone comes to see you, so you can let them in from anywhere.

Getting started

Ask your community manager for a pairing QR code, open the app, press Scan and point the camera at the code. Allow notifications so that your phone can ring when the app is closed.

Help

For your account, your phones or the gate itself, ask your community manager. For problems with the app, write to aigateambassador@gmail.com.
